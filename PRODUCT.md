# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Quantitative forex traders, portfolio managers, risk controllers, and autonomous algorithmic trading supervisors operating in live and paper-trading environments.

## Product Purpose

An institutional-grade algorithmic forex trading command center that unites multi-agent market intelligence, order flow verification, regime detection, and real-time execution safety into a high-density, low-latency control station.

## Positioning

Quantitative rigor and measured reality without fabricated metrics or AI-generated visual fluff. Every performance figure, drawdown, win rate, and agent verdict is backed by concrete logs and measured backtests as codified in AGENTS.md.

## Operating Context

- Real-time trading sessions across major FX pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD).
- MetaTrader 5 (MT5) gateway integration with millisecond order execution safety.
- Multi-agent debate and agent council shadow verdicts.
- Live system integrity and automated code-healing monitors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Live Capabilities:** Market Analyst signal generation, HMM Regime identification, Risk Gateway guardrails, Monitor agent exit controls, Learning Agent parameter tuning.
- **Durable Constraints:** Rule 1 of AGENTS.md holds universally: every number must be measured. No fake metrics, no bypass of risk limits, shadow council mode preserved until out-of-sample statistical proof is established.
- **Interface Constraint:** High-density, high-contrast numeric legibility. Visuals must serve instant decision-making. No purple-to-blue decorative gradients or nested card clutter.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice:** Quantitative, sober, precise, audit-ready.
- **Aesthetic:** Institutional dark trading terminal. High information density, clear visual hierarchy, disciplined financial status signaling.

## Evidence on Hand

- Core Python engine in `backend/` (`agents/`, `core/`, `models/`).
- Verified regression suites in `tests/` (16 passing tests).
- React/TypeScript frontend components in `src/components/` and `platform/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Measured Reality First:** Never present an unverified backtest figure or simulated certainty as live truth.
2. **Operational Scanability:** Financial metrics, active exposure, and agent signals must be parseable at a glance within 250ms.
3. **Fail-Closed Safety:** Risk limits, disconnection alerts, and kill-switches take absolute visual and functional priority.
4. **Restraint Over Decoration:** Clean typography and purposeful status colors beat decorative animations and visual clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliant contrast for all numeric data and financial indicators against dark backgrounds.
- High-contrast tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for price and PnL columns.
- Clear non-color indicators (icons, labels, and shapes) accompanying green/red status states to accommodate color vision deficiencies.
